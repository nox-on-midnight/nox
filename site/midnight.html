Midnight 101

The privacy blockchain in five minutes, explained by a moth. No prior crypto knowledge needed.

What Midnight is

Midnight is a blockchain built for data protection. It was developed by Input Output, the engineering company behind Cardano, and runs as a partner chain to Cardano.

Its genesis block was produced on 17 March 2026, and the network made its public debut at the end of that month after a testing period.

Why a blockchain needs privacy

Most blockchains are public by design. Anyone can look up a wallet and see its balance and every transaction it ever made. That's fine for a meme, and terrible for a salary, a medical record or a company's supplier deals.

Older "privacy coins" took the opposite route and hid everything, which keeps running into trouble with exchanges and regulators. Midnight aims for the middle, which it calls rational privacy: data is private by default, and you can still prove what needs proving.

Zero-knowledge proofs

A zero-knowledge proof lets you convince someone a statement is true without showing them the information behind it. Think of Nox's orb: he can prove it's real without letting you look inside.

Example: getting into a bar

The questionIs this person 18 or older?
Showing an ID cardReveals your name, birth date, address and photo
Showing a ZK proofReveals only yes

Midnight uses zero-knowledge proofs inside its smart contracts, so apps can check rules about your data without publishing the data itself.

Selective disclosure

Privacy doesn't have to be all or nothing. An app on Midnight can decide exactly what gets revealed, to whom and when. An auditor might see one specific record, a regulator might see a compliance check, and everyone else sees nothing.

That's Nox with one wing open: he chooses which part to show.

NIGHT and DUST

Midnight splits the jobs most blockchains give to a single coin between two things:

  • NIGHT is Midnight's native and governance token. It is public (unshielded), so balances can be seen like on other chains. It launched on Cardano in December 2025 and exists natively on both Cardano and Midnight.
  • DUST is the resource that pays for transactions and smart contracts. It is shielded and can't be transferred. You don't buy it: holding NIGHT generates it.

Midnight compares this to a rechargeable battery. You don't spend your NIGHT to use the network; it keeps producing DUST, which gets used up and refills. App builders can also hold NIGHT to cover DUST for their users, so those users don't need to hold any tokens at all.

And yes, that's why Nox is a moth: moth wings are covered in tiny dust-like scales, and he sheds DUST wherever he flies.

Compact, the developer language

Midnight smart contracts are written in Compact, a language based on TypeScript. It lets a single contract manage both public and private data, and developers don't need to be cryptography experts to use zero-knowledge proofs with it.
