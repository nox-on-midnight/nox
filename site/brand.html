Brand kit

Nox is free to meme. Download him, remix him, put him on a sticker. Just credit NightBringer and keep him masked.

Artwork

PNGs are 1024 px with transparent backgrounds. Telegram stickers need 512 px; resize before uploading.

Colors

Select a hex code to copy it.

  • Night InkBackgrounds
  • Wing IndigoWings and cloak
  • Wing RimOutlines and veins
  • Moth LavenderFur and body
  • Moon CreamCrescents, eyes, text
  • Dust GoldDust, orb, links

Type

Proves everything.

Bricolage Grotesque, free on Google Fonts. Headlines use the condensed width at weight 800; body text uses the normal width at weight 400.

Using Nox

Go ahead

  • Draw him in new poses, outfits and scenes
  • Use him to explain a Midnight idea; that's what he's for
  • Keep the mask, the wing crescents and the gold dust

Please don't

  • Pair him with Midnight's official logo or suggest an official partnership
  • Use him for price predictions or "guaranteed" gains
  • Use him to brand another token
