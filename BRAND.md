# NightBringer brand kit

## The mascot: Nox, the masked night moth

**Tagline:** Proves everything. Shows nothing.

A plump lavender moth with a dark domino mask, indigo wings marked with cream crescent moons, a fluffy cream ruff and a glowing golden orb held in his paws. Golden dust follows him everywhere.

| Feature | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Mask | Privacy by default |
| Wings folded / open | Shielded / disclosed (selective disclosure) |
| Golden orb | A zero-knowledge proof |
| Dust | NIGHT generates DUST |
| Crescents on the forewings | Midnight's moon-phase roadmap |

### Expressions

| File | Name | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `nox-shielded.svg` | Shielded | Privacy talk, "no comment", hiding |
| `nox-selective-disclosure.svg` | Selective disclosure | Sharing just enough, winks, teasers |
| `nox-wen-moon.svg` | Wen moon | Hype, anticipation, staring at the moon |
| `nox-dust-drop.svg` | Dust drop | Celebrations, NIGHT and DUST explainers |

All artwork lives in `site/assets/brand/`: SVG masters, plus 1024 px PNG exports in `png/`. The main character is `nox.svg`, the round profile picture `nox-avatar.svg`, and the social preview image `site/assets/og.png`.

The same kit, with downloads and click-to-copy colors, is on the website's brand kit page (`site/brand.html`).

## Colors

| Name | Hex | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Night Ink | `#14132B` | Background |
| Night Ink 2 | `#1E1C44` | Raised surfaces, cards |
| Wing Indigo | `#2E2B6B` | Wings, cloak |
| Wing Rim | `#6A63D6` | Outlines, veins, borders |
| Moth Lavender | `#A99BEA` | Fur, body |
| Moon Cream | `#F3ECD9` | Crescents, eyes, main text |
| Dust Gold | `#E8B64C` | Dust, orb, buttons, accents |

Gold is for dust and light only. Don't use it for large backgrounds.

## Type

- **Bricolage Grotesque** (Google Fonts) for everything. Headlines use the condensed width (75%) at weight 800; body text uses the normal width at weight 400.
- Monospace only for things that really are code, such as the policy ID.

## Rules

**Do**
- Keep the mask on. Always.
- Keep the crescents on the forewings.
- Remix freely: new scenes, outfits, poses.
- Credit NightBringer when you share (CC BY 4.0).

**Don't**
- Use Midnight's official logo or wordmark, or imply a partnership or endorsement.
- Put price predictions or promises of returns next to Nox.
- Use Nox to impersonate the project (fake airdrops, fake support accounts).
